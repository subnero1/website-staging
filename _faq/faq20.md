If you are an OEM developer, you can use [UnetCube](https://subnero.com/products/wnc/unetstack/unetcube) from Subnero and design your modem hardware around it. Supply the data acquisition and analog side, connect it to UnetCube over Ethernet using the open-source UASP protocol, and UnetCube handles the signal processing and the complete network stack above it. UnetCube can also add UnetStack networking on top of a third-party modem you already own.

If you create a modem based on UnetStack, we'd love to hear from you! Contact us at sales@subnero.com to have your project showcased on our social media channels.

For a detailed description of both approaches, refer to Section 6.3, "Building Your Own Modem Around UnetCube", of the [UnetStack whitepaper](https://subnero.com/brochures/unlocking-underwater-connectivity.pdf).
